## Getting Started

### Account Types

| Role | Description | Access |
|------|-------------|--------|
| **Student** | Classes 6–12 learners | Study, quizzes, progress, gamification, challenges, Refer & Earn |
| **Teacher** | School or independent teachers | Class join links, quiz creation, homework status and reminders, paper generation, student monitoring, School Ops (fees, attendance, timetable, gradebook, TC, notices) |
| **Parent** | Parents/guardians | View linked children's progress and their school details (attendance, fees, report cards, notices, homework) |
| **Principal** | Your school's principal (set up for you) | Today at my school, School Ops, parent logins on printed slips |
| **Admin** | AskAide platform administrators | Full school, teacher, student, content management |

### First-Time Setup

1. Visit the AskAide AI platform URL
2. Click **Sign Up** (or open a link someone shared with you: a friend's invite, a friend's challenge, or your teacher's class link)
3. Sign up with your email or with **Continue with Google**. You are signed in straight away
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete your profile (class, subjects, school)

> **Students:** If your teacher shared a class link (it looks like `.../join/ABC123`), open it and tap **Join the class**. If you don't have an account yet, sign up first; you are added to the class when you come back to the link page.

> **Students invited by a friend:** Sign up from your friend's invite link or challenge, and the invite is remembered even if you sign up later on the same device. See [Refer & Earn](#refer--earn).

> **Teachers:** You can create your own free teacher account (see [Teacher Signup](#teacher-signup)). If your school already set you up, use the login details or invite link from your school admin.

> **Who can sign up:** Only **Student** and **Teacher** accounts can be created at sign-up. Parent, principal and admin accounts are set up by your school or by AskAide.

**Passwords:** a new password (at sign-up, when you reset it, or when you change it) needs at least 8 characters, including at least one letter and one number.

### Study Sessions

Study sessions are the core learning experience. You practice MCQ and fill-in-the-blank questions tailored to your chapter and difficulty level.

#### Starting a Study Session

1. From the sidebar, click **Study Sessions** (or **Start Studying** from the dashboard)
2. **Step 1 — Select Class:** Choose your class level (6–12)
3. **Step 2 — Select Subject:** Pick a subject (e.g., Mathematics)
4. **Step 3 — Select Chapter:** Browse chapters within the subject and pick one (e.g., "Number Systems")
5. **Step 4 — Select Difficulty:**
   - **Easy** — Fundamentals and basics
   - **Medium** — Application-level questions
   - **Hard** — Advanced and competitive exam-level questions
6. Click **Start Session**

> **PLACEHOLDER: `[Screenshot of study session setup — class → subject → chapter → difficulty]`**

#### During a Study Session

- Questions appear one at a time
- **MCQ:** Select the correct option from A/B/C/D
- **Fill-in-the-blank:** Type your answer in the text field
- After answering, you'll see:
  - ✅ Correct / ❌ Incorrect indicator
  - **Explanation** for the correct answer
  - **Related topic** link for deeper study
- Click **Next** to proceed to the next question

#### Session Summary

At the end of each session:
- Total questions attempted
- Accuracy percentage
- Time taken
- Breakdown by topic/difficulty
- Points earned
- Any badge you just earned (shown first, then the result card)
- A **Challenge on WhatsApp** button (**Challenge a friend** on small phones), pinned at the bottom of the result card so you can tap it without scrolling. See [Challenge a Friend](#challenge-a-friend).

Every answer is saved the moment you give it, so closing the tab mid-session doesn't lose your progress.

### Student Quizzes

Quizzes assigned by your teachers appear here.

#### Viewing Assigned Quizzes

1. Click **Quizzes** in the sidebar (on phones it's the **Quizzes** tab in the bottom bar)
2. You'll see only quizzes your teachers have published for your class and subject. Each card shows one status:
   - **Available** — Ready to attempt
   - **In Progress** — Started but not submitted
   - **Completed** — You have submitted at least one attempt (your best score is shown)
   - **Expired** — The deadline passed before you attempted it
3. A quiz due within a day shows **Due today!** or **Due tomorrow** instead
4. The counters at the top show how many quizzes are Available, In Progress and Completed. You can search by title and filter by status

> **PLACEHOLDER: `[Screenshot of quiz list with status badges]`**

#### Taking a Quiz

1. Click **Start Quiz** on an available quiz (or **Resume Quiz** on one you already started)
2. Answer questions — navigate freely between questions
3. Click **Submit** when done (or it auto-submits when the timer runs out)

Your answers are saved as you go. If you reload the page or come back later, the quiz picks up where you left off, with your answers and the questions in the same order.

If the teacher allows more than one attempt, a completed quiz shows **Results** and **Retry**. When no attempts are left, it shows **View Results**, or says the deadline has passed.

#### Viewing Quiz Results

After submission:
- Score and percentage, and whether you passed (the teacher sets the pass mark, 50% by default)
- Number of correct answers and the time you spent
- Question-by-question review with the correct answers and explanations. Your teacher decides when answers are shown: right after you submit, after the deadline, or never
- **Try Again** (when you have attempts left and the deadline hasn't passed) starts a new attempt straight away
- **More Quizzes** goes back to the quiz list, and **Quiz History** opens your past attempts

#### Quiz History

- The **Quiz History** page (`/quiz/history`) lists your submitted attempts, newest first. Open it with the **History** button at the top of the quiz list, or **Quiz History** on a result page
- Search by quiz title
- Open any attempt to see its result again

### Gamification

AskAide AI keeps you motivated through gamification features.

#### Daily Streaks

- Study every day to build your streak
- Each consecutive day adds +1 to your streak
- Missing a day resets the streak (unless you use a **Streak Freeze**)
- If you practised in the last 7 days but not yet today, you get a streak reminder in the bell each evening at 5 pm (India time). See [Notifications](#notifications)

#### Streak Freezes

- You get a free streak freeze every week (it resets on Monday)
- Extra freezes, called **streak shields**, come with the [Refer & Earn](#refer--earn) gift. They never expire and are used only after your weekly freeze
- Each freeze protects your streak for one missed day
- View your available freezes in the dashboard

#### Badges

| Badge | How to Earn |
|-------|-------------|
| 🎯 **First Steps** | Complete your first practice session |
| 📚 **Quick Learner** | Complete 10 sessions in a week |
| ⏱️ **Warm Up** / 📖 **Getting Serious** / 🐛 **Bookworm** / 🎓 **Dedicated Student** | Study for 1 / 10 / 20 / 40 hours in total |
| ⭐ **Perfect Score** | Score 100% in a practice session |
| 🧮 **Math Master** | Score 90%+ in 5 Math sessions in a row |
| 🦉 **Night Owl** / 🌅 **Early Bird** | Study between midnight and 5 AM / between 5 AM and 7 AM (India time) |
| 🔥 **Streak Starter** / ⚡ **Week Warrior** / 🏆 **Streak Master** | Reach a 7 / 14 / 30-day practice streak |
| 🎮 **Weekend Warrior** | Complete 5 weekend study sessions |
| 👑 **Comeback King** | Come back after 7+ days away |
| 🌍 **Subject Explorer** | Master 5 chapters in one subject with 70%+ accuracy |
| ⚔️ **Challenger** | A friend played your challenge |
| 🥇 **Challenge Champion** | Beat a friend's challenge score |
| 🤝 **Squad Starter** / 🫂 **Squad Leader** / 🎖️ **Class Captain** | 1 / 3 / 5 friends you invited are practising |

#### Leaderboards

- The dashboard's **Leaderboard** card ("Top learners this week") shows the top 10 students of the current week
- The week starts on Monday (India time), so everyone starts fresh each week and new students can catch up
- Students are ranked by correct answers this week and shown by first name only

#### Daily Goals

- Set a daily study goal (e.g., 30 minutes, 20 questions)
- Track progress toward your goal on the dashboard
- Earn bonus points for meeting your goal consistently

### Challenge a Friend

Turn any practice session into a quiz your friends can play, then see who scored more.

1. Finish a practice session. On the result card, tap **Challenge on WhatsApp** (on small phones the button says **Challenge a friend**). The button is pinned at the bottom of the card, so you don't need to scroll
2. WhatsApp opens with a ready message and your challenge link (it looks like `.../c/ABC123`). Send it to a friend or a class group. You need at least 3 multiple-choice answers in the session to make a challenge
3. Your friend opens the link and plays the same questions. **They don't need an account to play**
4. Your friend sees right away whether they won, lost or tied, and where they rank among everyone who played
5. To see the correct answers and explanations, your friend signs in (or signs up). Their score is saved to the new account automatically
6. You get a notification each time someone plays your challenge. Open it to see the results page with every player's score

**Good to know:**
- Each friend's first score is the one that counts; playing again shows the same result
- You can't play your own challenge
- A friend who signs up after playing your challenge counts as a friend you invited, and their challenge answers count towards your [Refer & Earn](#refer--earn) gift
- Badges: **Challenger** when a friend plays your challenge, **Challenge Champion** when you beat a friend's score

### Refer & Earn

Invite friends to AskAide. When a friend you invited answers **3 questions**, you **both** get a gift:

- 🎁 **A free practice paper**: a 20-question practice paper with answer key for any chapter you pick, downloadable as a PDF
- 🛡️ **A streak shield**: an extra streak freeze that saves your streak if you miss a day

**How to invite:**
1. Open **Refer & Earn** (from the invite card on your dashboard or profile, tap **Details →**), or tap **Invite on WhatsApp** on the card
2. Share your invite link. A friend can also join through a challenge you sent (see [Challenge a Friend](#challenge-a-friend))
3. Your friend signs up from your link (email or Google)
4. When your friend has answered 3 questions, you both get the gift. Answers in challenges they played count too

**On the Refer & Earn page you can:**
- Share your invite on WhatsApp or copy the invite message
- See each friend you invited and whether they have unlocked the gift yet
- Under **Your gifts**, get your practice paper: pick a chapter and tap **Get my practice paper (PDF)** (if the paper can't be made for that chapter, you keep your credit)
- See progress towards the **Squad Starter**, **Squad Leader** and **Class Captain** badges
- See the challenges you've sent and who played them
- If a friend invited you, see how many of the 3 questions you need you've done

> **Note:** Invites work for new accounts only. If you signed up with a friend's invite, your friend is credited automatically.

### Notifications

The **bell** shows what's new: a friend played your challenge, a friend joined with your invite, your gift unlocked, you're a few questions away from your gift, you earned a badge, your streak needs you tonight, or a teacher has sent you a homework reminder. Teachers also see students joining their class links and their class report or certificate becoming ready.

- **Where the bell is:** in the sidebar on desktop, in the top bar on public pages, and next to the greeting on the student dashboard on phones. On phones, the **Menu** tab in the bottom bar also shows the number of unread notifications, and the menu has a **Notifications** row
- A red number on the bell shows how many are unread. It updates about every minute while AskAide is open
- Tap the bell to open the list (a sheet from the top on phones, a panel next to the bell on desktop). Opening it marks everything as read. Tap a notification to go to the related page
- **Streak reminder:** each evening at 5 pm (India time), if you practised in the last 7 days but not today, you get one reminder. It says **Your N-day streak ends tonight** if your streak is still alive, or **Pick up where you left off** if it has lapsed. Tapping it opens Study
- **Homework reminder:** when a teacher reminds you about a quiz set as homework, you get one reminder for that quiz, with its due day. Tapping it opens your quizzes
- Similar notifications from the same day are grouped, for example "Kabir and 2 others played your challenge"
- When something new arrives while you're using the app, a short pop-up appears. Pop-ups stay quiet while you're in a practice session or a quiz
- Notifications are kept for 60 days

### Student Profile

1. Click **Profile** in the sidebar
2. View your:
   - Name, class, school
   - Total points, badges, streak
   - Subjects and mastery overview
3. Click **Edit Profile** to update:
   - Profile picture
   - Class/subject changes

#### Changing Your Name

1. On your **Profile**, tap **Edit** next to your name
2. Type the new name (2–100 characters) and save

Your name is what friends and teachers see (for example on the leaderboard and in challenges). It isn't used to sign in, so no confirmation is needed.

#### Changing Your Email

Your email is used to sign in, so changing it takes a confirmation code:

1. On your **Profile**, tap **Change** next to your email
2. Enter the new email address. AskAide sends a 6-digit code **to the new address**
3. Enter the code within 10 minutes. Your sign-in email switches to the new address, and your old address gets a message saying the email was changed

Until you enter the code, nothing changes on your account. If the code expired or you entered it wrong too many times, ask for a new one (you can request a new code after a minute). An email address that another account already uses can't be chosen.

> These name and email options are on the Profile page for every account type, not just students.

#### Public Profile

- Anyone with the link to your public profile can open it, without signing in
- Shows your name, picture, current and best streak, questions practised, accuracy and number of subjects (no email or other private details)
- There is no setting to hide it

### Free Paper Generator (No Signup)

Generate exam papers instantly — no account required.

1. Click **Free Paper Generator** on the homepage
2. Select **class** and **subject**
3. Choose **chapters** to include — the generator shows how many questions of each type they have ready: MCQ, fill-in-the-blank, assertion-reason, 2-mark, 3-mark, 5-mark and case-based
4. Pick **question types and counts** (up to 30 questions in total). With written questions ready for your chapters, the untouched default is a 20-mark class test
5. Click **Generate**

   > If you ask for more of a type than those chapters have ready, the paper says so in plain words ("Only 1 five-mark question is ready for these chapters."). MCQ and fill-in questions fill each other's place; the written types never.

6. **Edit the paper** on the hand-made school paper view — swap any question for another of the same type, change text, marks or answers, add your own question, or set the header (exam name, session, class & section, date, time); CBSE-style instructions are written for you
7. **Download** the PDF (or the editable Word file) and print — every paper carries a separate answer key, with the model solution and value points for written questions

> **Note:** Assertion-reason, 2/3/5-mark and case-based questions are written specially for paper printing and are shown only after they have been reviewed; a new chapter starts with just MCQ and fill-in-the-blank. A generator draft is kept on your device, so a reload or a later signup resumes it.

> **Tip:** Signing in (free) keeps your papers in history, autosaves your edits and lets you share the paper as a PDF.

### Class Join Links

A class link brings a whole class into AskAide with one message. Students who join appear in your Teacher Dashboard, just like students your school links to you.

**Creating a link:**
1. On the Teacher Dashboard, tap **Create your class link** (new teachers) or **Invite students**. This opens the **Class links** page
2. Pick the **class** and **subject**. Optionally add a **section** (for example "B") and how many students you expect
3. Share the link: send it on **WhatsApp** (a ready message in English and Hindi for the class or parents' group), **copy** it, or show the **QR code** on the projector

Making a link again for the same class, subject and section gives you the same link.

**How students join:**
1. A student opens the link (it looks like `.../join/ABC123`) and sees your name, the class and the subject
2. A signed-in student taps **Join the class**. A student without an account signs up (email or Google) and is added to your class automatically when they come back to the link
3. You get a notification when students join

Only student accounts can join a class link.

**Tracking your class:** the Class links page shows, for each link, how many students joined, how many have practised since joining, and how many were active this week. A printable **class progress report** and an **AskAide Champion Teacher certificate** unlock as more of your students practise; the page shows your progress towards each, and you get a notification when one is ready.

**Closing a link:** turn a link off on the Class links page when you no longer want new students to join. Students who already joined stay in your class. You can turn it back on later.

### Quiz Management

#### Creating a Quiz

1. Click **Quizzes → Create New Quiz**
2. Fill in quiz details:
   - **Title** (e.g., "Chapter 3 — Algebra Test")
   - **Class & Subject**
   - **Time Limit** (in minutes)
   - **Total Marks**
   - **Instructions** (optional)
3. Add questions using one of these methods:

**Option A — From Question Bank:**
1. Click **Add from Question Bank**
2. Filter by chapter, topic, difficulty
3. Select questions (checkboxes)
4. Click **Add Selected**

**Option B — Custom Questions:**
1. Click **Add Custom Question**
2. Choose type: MCQ or Fill-in-the-blank
3. Enter question text, options/correct answer, and explanation
4. Repeat for each question

4. **Reorder questions** by dragging (optional)
5. Click **Save as Draft** or **Publish**

> **PLACEHOLDER: `[Screenshot of quiz creation form]`**

#### Publishing a Quiz

- **Draft** → **Published** makes it visible to students
- Set an **available from** and **deadline** date/time
- Students see it in their quiz list once published

#### Quiz Analytics

After students attempt the quiz:
1. Click **Analytics** on the quiz card
2. View:
   - Average score, highest/lowest scores
   - Per-question accuracy (% who got it right)
   - Time distribution
   - Student-by-student breakdown

#### Homework Status & Reminders

Once a quiz is published, open its **Analytics** page to see a **Homework status** list: each student in the class, with their status for this quiz:

- **Done**: submitted
- **In progress**: started, not yet submitted
- **Not done**: not started
- **Never logged in**: has not signed in yet

Each row shows when the student was last reminded. If you have the parent's number, a **WhatsApp** button opens a ready message to the parent with the quiz title and deadline.

- **Remind students:** the **Remind** button sends one in-app reminder to each student who has not finished the quiz. A student is reminded once per quiz, so tapping again is safe. The page tells you how many were already reminded. Reminders can't be sent for an unpublished quiz or after its deadline
- **Give more time:** next to the due date, tap **Give more time** and pick a later deadline. The new deadline must be in the future and later than the current one. Students who were already reminded see the new due day in their bell

#### Cloning a Quiz

- Click the **Clone** icon on any existing quiz
- A copy is created as a draft
- Modify as needed and publish for another class

### Question Paper Generator

Generate exam-ready question papers with a custom difficulty mix, or exact counts per question type.

1. Click **Paper Generator** in the sidebar
2. Configure:
   - **Class & Subject**
   - **Chapters** to include — see how many questions of each type are ready
   - **Question Types and Counts** — MCQ, fill-in-the-blank, assertion-reason, 2/3/5-mark and case-based (up to 30 questions; written types are never topped up from another type), or a difficulty distribution (Easy ___% / Medium ___% / Hard ___%)
3. Click **Generate Paper**
4. **Preview** the generated paper on the hand-made school paper view (sections A/B, continuous numbering, Set A + Set B)
5. **Edit** right there — swap, edit or move questions, add your own, fix marks and answers — it autosaves
6. **Download PDF** (the PDF embeds a print-safe font — a DejaVu Serif Condensed subset — so symbols like √, π, ∠ and H₂O print correctly) or the editable **Word** file
7. **Share** the paper as a PDF; the separate answer key is available for download

## School Ops

School Ops runs a school's day-to-day work in one place: fees, the day book, attendance, timetable, gradebook, transfer certificates, notices and parent logins. Open it from the **School Ops** group in the sidebar (on phones, from **Menu**). Each screen has its own tabs across the top.

### Who Can Do What

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| **Teacher** | Collect fees and see the day book, mark attendance, enter marks, see your own timetable and cover duties, request transfer certificates, post notices |
| **Principal** | Everything a teacher can do, plus refunds, fee concessions, the staff register, approving cover, publishing report cards, overriding the dues block on a transfer certificate, and parent logins |
| **Admin** | Everything, plus setup: fee heads and fee structures, exam plans, timetable setup, and bulk student import |

### Principal Today

The principal's home screen (**Principal** in the sidebar) shows **Today at [school name]**. It has cards for attendance today, fees (day book and defaulters), staff today, and transfer certificates this month. Each card links to the screen where you act on it.

### Fees & Receipts

The Fees screen has **Collect**, **Billing**, **Receipts** and, for admins, **Setup** tabs.

- **Collect:** see who owes fees, then **Record cash payment**. You can record cash, a bank transfer (enter the UTR number), or UPI (confirm later)
- **Advance and several months:** one payment can cover several months or pay ahead for later months. The school gets one receipt for the whole payment
- **Billing:** generate invoices for a fee structure
- **Receipts:** look up any receipt by its number and print it. Principals can refund a payment (a reason of at least 5 characters is required) and apply a concession
- **Day book:** the day's collections and receipts, on its own screen

### Attendance, Timetable & Gradebook

- **Attendance:** **Mark** the day's register (and period marking where the school uses it). **Day view** shows the day's absentees. The **Staff** register is for principals
- **Timetable:** the **Cover** tab (principals) shows who is absent and who is free to cover, and lets the principal approve covers. **Setup** (admin) holds the weekly slot grid
- **Gradebook:** teachers use **Enter marks** for exams. Admins set up **Plans** (exam plans). Principals publish **Report cards**, which then appear on parents' School card

### Transfer Certificates

- A teacher or principal requests a transfer certificate (TC) for a student who is leaving. Each TC gets the next number for the school
- If the student still has unpaid dues, the request is blocked. A principal can override the block; every override is recorded
- Print the TC from the **TC** tab

### Parent Logins & Notices

**Parent logins** (principal and admin) give each family a login on a printed slip. No email or SMS is sent.

1. Open **Parent logins**. Each family shows one of three statuses: **No login yet**, **Slip given, not used yet**, or **Using AskAide**
2. Tap **Make & print** slips for families with no login. Families who haven't logged in yet can be given a new slip too
3. Slips print eight to an A4 page. Cut along the dashed lines and send them home
4. Brothers and sisters share one login. Families already using AskAide are left alone

Passwords are shown only once, on the printed slips, so print them straight away. A **New slip** gives a family a new password. The old slip stops working, and anyone signed in with it is signed out.

**Notices** (principal and teachers) post a message for parents. It appears on each child's School card, and the page shows how many parents have seen it ("seen by X of Y").

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| **Can't login** | Check email/password, reset password via "Forgot Password" |
| **No quizzes showing** | Ensure your teacher has published the quiz and assigned it to your class |
| **Chapter not processing** | Check admin panel for error status; re-upload the PDF |
| **Slow page load** | Clear browser cache, check internet connection |
| **Parent can't see child** | Ask your school office to check that your child is on a class list and that your parent login is linked to them |
| **Parent slip login doesn't work** | Type the Login ID and Password exactly as printed. If the slip is lost or old, ask the school office for a new one |
| **Can't sign up as a parent or principal** | Only Student and Teacher accounts can be created at sign-up. Ask your school office for your account |
| **Password rejected** | A new password needs at least 8 characters, including one letter and one number |
| **AI generates wrong content** | Edit the generated content before publishing; provide more specific prompts |
| **Quiz results not showing** | Ensure quiz is submitted (not just saved); check analytics tab |
| **Streak not updating** | Streak requires completing at least one question; login alone doesn't count |
| **Can't make a challenge** | Answer at least 3 multiple-choice questions in the session first |
| **Refer & Earn gift not unlocked yet** | The gift unlocks for both of you once your friend has answered 3 questions (practice and challenge answers both count). Open **Refer & Earn** to see the latest progress |
| **Can't join a class link** | Only student accounts can join. If the page says the link is turned off, ask your teacher for a new one |
| **Email change code not arriving** | Check the spam folder of the **new** address; you can ask for a new code after a minute. Codes expire after 10 minutes |
